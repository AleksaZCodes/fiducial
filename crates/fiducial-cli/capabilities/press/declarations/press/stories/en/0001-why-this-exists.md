  A STORY is not a blog post and not a press release.

  It is raw material for someone else's article: the facts, the sequence, the
  quote, and the tension — written so a journalist can take an angle from it
  without having to interview you first to find one.

  `angle` is the frontmatter field that matters. It names the story this piece
  supports, so a reporter scanning the index can find the one that fits the
  section they write for. Useful values: origin, technical, customer, failure,
  milestone, people, market.

  **Include the failure ones.** A press room with only wins reads as marketing
  and gets used as marketing — which is to say, not used. The story about what
  did not work is the one that gets a call back, because it is the only one
  that suggests you will answer a hard question honestly.

  Write the quote you would actually say out loud, attributed to a named
  person. An unattributed quote cannot be printed.
