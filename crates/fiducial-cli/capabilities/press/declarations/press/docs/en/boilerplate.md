<!--
  The boilerplate: the paragraph a journalist pastes at the bottom of a piece.

  Three lengths, because three are what get asked for and writing a fourth on
  deadline is how an inaccurate one enters circulation. Once a wrong number is
  in one article it is in every article that cites it.

  Write them as plain fact. No adjectives that cannot be checked, no "leading",
  no "revolutionary". A journalist who has to strip your adjectives will strip
  your meaning with them, and a claim you cannot support is the one a sceptical
  editor will pick.

  If this product has a claims policy, these sentences are subject to it.
-->

Example builds an example. It was founded in 2026 and is based nowhere in
particular.
