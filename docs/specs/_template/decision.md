## Context

What was true that made this necessary. Include the **evidence** — a number, a
file, a failure. A context section that could have been written without looking
at the repository is not a context section.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice.

## Why not the alternatives

The options rejected, and what each would have cost. This is the section a
future reader actually needs: they will arrive already knowing what was chosen
and wanting to know why the obvious alternative was not.

## Consequences

What becomes true, including what becomes harder.

## What this deliberately does not do

Stating the boundary stops it being re-proposed every six months.
